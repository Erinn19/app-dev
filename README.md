## About the Movie

*Pee Mak* is a horror-comedy about a soldier who returns home to his wife, but his friends suspect she is a ghost. It is based on the Thai folktale of **Mae Nak Phra Khanong**.

> The Philippines recently made its own adaptation of this movie.

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Original Version | Philippine Version |
| :----- | :--------------- | :----------------- |
| Country | Thailand | Philippines |
| Year | 2013 | 2026 |
| Leads | Mario Maurer, Davika Hoorne | Wilbert Ross, Bea Binene |
